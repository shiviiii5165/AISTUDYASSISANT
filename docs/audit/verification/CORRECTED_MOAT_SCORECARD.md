## 2. Feature-by-Feature Deep Differentiator Specification (Gate 20)

### 1. Presentation Engine (PPT)
- **PROBLEM**: Educational slides generated by AI are visually cluttered bullet-dumps that students cannot read during lectures.
- **CURRENT MARKET SOLUTION**: Gamma and Canva format generic bulleted cards with stock photos.
- **WHAT REMAINS UNSOLVED**: Dual-coding alignment (visual diagram directly reinforcing verbal concept), cognitive load budgets (<45 words/slide), and misconception contrast cards.
- **TEACHGENIE MECHANISM**: **Pedagogical Slide Grammar & Understanding Compiler**.
- **ALGORITHM**: Cognitive archetype classification (`MISCONCEPTION_CONTRAST`, `WORKED_EXAMPLE`, `CAUSAL_SYSTEM`).
- **DATA REQUIRED**: Topic ontology subtopics, student reading level, curriculum board.
- **LLM REQUIRED**: 2 Fast Structured Calls (Ontology extraction + Slide content population).
- **NON-LLM COMPUTATION**: Deterministic CSS/SVG geometry linters, banned-string scans, density budgeting, and PPTX native XML compilation.
- **EXPECTED OUTCOME**: Zero text overflow bugs; >85% teacher presentation acceptance without manual edits.
- **HOW WE MEASURE**: Deterministic layout QA pass rate + 100-point pedagogical rubric score.
- **HOW COMPETITOR COULD COPY**: Could copy slide layouts, but cannot dynamically emphasize the class's active quiz misconceptions without our response graph.
- **WHAT BECOMES DEFENSIBLE OVER TIME**: Proprietary template geometry rules and domain-specific visual mapping libraries.

### 2. Diagnostic Quiz Engine
- **PROBLEM**: Standard AI multiple-choice questions have obvious, nonsensical wrong answers (distractors) that measure guessing skill rather than concept understanding.
- **CURRENT MARKET SOLUTION**: Quizlet / ChatGPT prompt: "Generate 4 multiple-choice options with 1 correct."
- **WHAT REMAINS UNSOLVED**: Explaining *why* a student picked option (C) and isolating their specific mental error.
- **TEACHGENIE MECHANISM**: **Misconception-Mapped Diagnostic Distractor Generator**.
- **ALGORITHM**: Item discrimination optimization against Knowledge Pack misconception nodes.
- **DATA REQUIRED**: Curriculum learning objective, known student cognitive bugs.
- **LLM REQUIRED**: 1 Structured Call with Pydantic schema mapping each distractor to an explicit fallacy code.
- **NON-LLM COMPUTATION**: Bloom taxonomy balance verification, duplicate stem detection, option randomization.
- **EXPECTED OUTCOME**: Single-question discrimination index increases from 0.28 to 0.74.
- **HOW WE MEASURE**: Point biserial correlation coefficient on student test cohorts.
- **HOW COMPETITOR COULD COPY**: Could prompt an LLM for "plausible distractors", but lacks a structured misconception ontology.
- **WHAT BECOMES DEFENSIBLE OVER TIME**: Empirical database of item response performance (IRT) calibrated across thousands of student attempts.

### 3. Live Classroom Quiz
- **PROBLEM**: Teachers see raw response percentages (e.g., "42% got Question 3 wrong") during live class but lack the time to diagnose and prepare an immediate reteaching explanation.
- **CURRENT MARKET SOLUTION**: Kahoot and Mentimeter show bar charts and music leaderboards.
- **WHAT REMAINS UNSOLVED**: Immediate instructional guidance on *how* to intervene in the next 90 seconds.
- **TEACHGENIE MECHANISM**: **Live Teaching Control System**.
- **ALGORITHM**: Real-time response clustering into dominant misconception buckets + automated intervention dispatch.
- **DATA REQUIRED**: Live WebSocket student response stream, distractor misconception mappings.
- **LLM REQUIRED**: 0 calls for visual diagrams; 1 sub-200 token call for a 90-second teacher explanation script.
- **NON-LLM COMPUTATION**: Atomic WebSocket state transitions, student speed bonus scoring, answer aggregation.
- **EXPECTED OUTCOME**: Class-wide error resolution rate increases by 65% within the same instructional period.
- **HOW WE MEASURE**: Immediate post-intervention verification question pass rate.
- **HOW COMPETITOR COULD COPY**: Can copy polling UI, but lacks pre-mapped misconception tags and real-time script generation.
- **WHAT BECOMES DEFENSIBLE OVER TIME**: Classroom interaction telemetry linking specific intervention scripts to successful remediation.

---

### 4. Interactive Socratic Tutor (TeachMe)
- **PROBLEM**: Chatbots lecture students with lengthy text paragraphs instead of discovering why they are actually confused.
- **CURRENT MARKET SOLUTION**: Khanmigo and ChatGPT Edu provide polite Socratic prompts.
- **WHAT REMAINS UNSOLVED**: Diagnosing broken foundational prerequisites (e.g., trying to explain integrals when the student cannot factor polynomials).
- **TEACHGENIE MECHANISM**: **Prerequisite-First Recursive Diagnostic Tutor**.
- **ALGORITHM**: Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) prerequisite traversal with Bayesian Knowledge Tracing updates.
- **DATA REQUIRED**: Concept prerequisite dependency graph, student historical mastery profile.
- **LLM REQUIRED**: 1 Fast Utterance Call (Groq `qwen/qwen3.8-27b` at <850ms latency).
- **NON-LLM COMPUTATION**: Deterministic checkpoint evaluation (MCQ/Regex), DAG navigation, BKT probability math.
- **EXPECTED OUTCOME**: Foundational misconceptions resolved in 3.2 turns on average vs. 8.4 turns in standard LLM chats.
- **HOW WE MEASURE**: Number of conversational turns required to achieve correct answer on independent transfer checkpoint.
- **HOW COMPETITOR COULD COPY**: Can build a Socratic prompt, but lacks a persistent DAG prerequisite traversal state machine.
- **WHAT BECOMES DEFENSIBLE OVER TIME**: Validated prerequisite graphs showing the exact dependency chains where students stumble.

### 5. AI Study Notes Generator
- **PROBLEM**: Generative notes produce monolithic summaries that re-explain topics students have already mastered.
- **CURRENT MARKET SOLUTION**: NotebookLM and Notion AI summarize pasted documents or textbook chapters.
- **WHAT REMAINS UNSOLVED**: Highlighting and repairing the student's unique individual learning gaps.
- **TEACHGENIE MECHANISM**: **Gap-Repair Notes (Cached Base + Dynamic Misconception Layer)**.
- **ALGORITHM**: Priority Context Resolution (`resolver.py`) + Dynamic Gap Extraction.
- **DATA REQUIRED**: Student quiz error history, active misconception tags, syllabus standards.
- **LLM REQUIRED**: 0 tokens for base notes (served from L2 cache); 1 sub-300 token call for the personalized repair callout.
- **NON-LLM COMPUTATION**: LaTeX validation, Flesch-Kincaid readability scoring, PDF geometry and pagination.
- **EXPECTED OUTCOME**: 40% reduction in student reading time with 100% coverage of active conceptual gaps.
- **HOW WE MEASURE**: Time spent reading vs. post-study quiz retention score.
- **HOW COMPETITOR COULD COPY**: Can summarize text, but has no student quiz history to build the personalized gap layer.
- **WHAT BECOMES DEFENSIBLE OVER TIME**: Pre-compiled, verified Knowledge Pack notes across all major school boards (CBSE, ICSE, NCERT).
