> **Truth Audit Disclosure**:

- **Blinded Recommendation Set IDs**: `REC_SET_01` through `REC_SET_15`

### Evaluated Criteria (0–100 Scale)
1. **Relevance**: Direct alignment with learning objective and canonical concepts.
2. **Authority**: Verifiable institutional pedigree (NPTEL, MIT, Stanford, official docs).
3. **Level Fit**: Cognitive load matching target learner grade/level.
4. **Usefulness**: Explanatory power and concrete problem-solving guidance.
5. **Freshness**: Currency of technical documentation and lack of obsolescence.
6. **Actionability**: Exactness of section/timestamp directives and clarity of next step.
